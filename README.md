# Camera Pulse – PPG recorder

Measures the pulse wave (photoplethysmography, PPG) through a fingertip with the phone camera
and flash, shows the heart rate and lets you download the signal and detected beats as CSV.

Open it on a phone: https://fri-linda.github.io/MI-hr-measurement/

Everything runs in the browser, no data leaves the device. For teaching purposes only
(course Medical Informatics, FRI UNIZA). Not a medical device.
