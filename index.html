    How to measure
    
    
      
  1. Press Start and allow camera access. The camera preview and the flash turn on.
    2. 
      
  2. Cover the rear camera and the flash with your fingertip. The preview should turn evenly red.
    3. 
      
  3. Press lightly. Pressing hard pushes the blood out and the signal disappears.
    4. 
      
  4. Rest your hand and the phone on a table and keep still. Recording starts by itself after the
        finger has been still for 2 seconds. The circle shows the camera and the time left.
    5. 
      
  5. The page records a short video and evaluates it afterwards; the result appears below.
    6. 
      
  6. Analyze a video: record 20 to 30 s with the phone's camera app instead (video mode,
        flash on, finger on the camera) and choose the file. The first second with a steady finger
        is skipped automatically.
    7. 
      
  7. If the flash does not turn on, tap Flash, try another camera, or shine a second phone's
        flashlight through your finger.
