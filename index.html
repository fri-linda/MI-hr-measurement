Signal CSV: every camera frame (time, mean red/green/blue, filtered pulse wave). Beats CSV: time of each detected beat, interval to the previous beat (RR) and instantaneous BPM.

How to measure
  1. Press Start and allow camera access. The camera preview and the flash turn on.
  2. Cover the rear camera and the flash with your fingertip. The preview should turn evenly red.
  3. Press lightly. Pressing hard pushes the blood out and the signal disappears.
  4. Rest your hand and the phone on a table and keep still. Measuring starts by itself after the finger has been still for 2 seconds.
  5. If the flash does not turn on, tap Flash, try another camera, or shine a second phone's flashlight through your finger.

For teaching purposes only. Not a medical device.
